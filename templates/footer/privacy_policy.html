Privacy policy

Effective date: 1 January 2025

Last updated: 10 February 2025

Welcome to Tracklio. Your privacy matters. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the platform. By using Tracklio, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We collect:

  • Account data: Username and hashed password when you register.
  • Expense data: Details you enter (dates, amounts, categories, notes).
  • Usage data: Such as device type, browser, IP address, and interaction patterns to improve security and the product.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To store and display your expense records and reports.
  • To improve features, performance, and security.

3. Data security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No online service can guarantee perfect security; we work continuously to reduce risk.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only when:

  • Working with service providers who help us run Tracklio under strict confidentiality.
  • Required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
  • Necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of Tracklio, users, or the public.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your account and associated data where applicable law allows.

6. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies for sessions, preferences, and analytics where enabled. You can control cookies through your browser; some features may not work if you disable them.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post changes here and update the effective date. Continued use after changes means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact us

For privacy questions, please contact us.
