About Tracklio

Personal expense tracking, built for clarity.

Our mission

At Tracklio, our mission is to simplify expense tracking for everyone. We believe managing your finances should be effortless, efficient, and accessible. Our goal is to provide you with a hassle-free tool to track, manage, and gain insights into your spending habits.

What we do

Tracklio is a personal expense tracker that lets you add, delete, and manage your expenses with ease. You can view monthly reports for any year and visualize your spending trends month by month, helping you understand and optimize your financial habits.

Why choose Tracklio?

  • Simple and intuitive: A clean, user-friendly interface designed for effortless expense tracking.
  • Secure and private: Your data is stored securely, and your financial information stays private.
  • Personalized reports: Visualize expenses by month and get detailed insights for any year.
  • Real-time tracking: Add and manage expenses whenever you need to.
  • Free to start: Core tracking is free, with optional plans as we grow.
